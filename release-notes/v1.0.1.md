**NetPulse 1.0.1** for Windows 10/11 (x64) · Free for personal use

### What's new

- The graph keeps its last hour of traffic through a restart, and app icons only show where there's traffic to go with them
- Time when NetPulse wasn't running is shown as a gap instead of being drawn over
- Custom range is now a timeline of your whole history — drag the edges, slide it, or pick any stretch (graph and Data usage)
- Data usage: "Yesterday" replaced with "3 days"
- Clear usage history now clears everything, including the graphs and this session's totals
- Fixed "Enable full monitoring" closing NetPulse without starting it again
- NetPulse only runs with administrator rights when you turn it on — installing it no longer does that for you
- Uninstalling with "delete my history" now removes it reliably, even if NetPulse was still running; fixed garbled characters in the installer
